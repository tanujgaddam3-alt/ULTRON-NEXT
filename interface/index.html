SOURCE VIDEO WORKFLOW

Transcription & Highlights

Upload only video you own or are authorized to edit. Private review does not publish or grant rights; existing publication controls remain separate.

Registered-source transcription and highlights

Check rights for an owner-registered asset, resume its transcription after rights clearance, and review returned transcript segments and highlight candidates here. Use CRE Overview for autonomous source-video editing.

Formal rights review and registered asset workflow

Rights have not been independently reviewed.

For submit_claim and create_agreement, omit evidenceBase64/agreementBase64 from the JSON; the selected file is encoded locally for that request. Use the existing rights steps in order: submit_claim, read_evidence, review_initial, register_authority, review_authority, create_agreement, review_agreement, record_grant. Submitter operations require the asset owner session; review operations require the separately configured reviewer principal. Decisions are never automatic.

Required fields for each rights step

All request objects include step. submit_claim: assetId, targetContext, accountId, territory, rightsHolder, authorityDescription, evidenceKind, evidenceReference, terms. read_evidence: evidenceId. review_initial: evidenceId, grantorId, decision, reason. register_authority: assetId, claimId, evidenceId, grantorId, rightsHolder, relationship, scopeDescription, evidenceReference, evidenceSha256. review_authority: authorityId, decision, evidenceSha256, reason. create_agreement: evidenceReference, terms, grantorId, authorityId, assetId, rightsHolder. review_agreement: agreementId, agreementVersion, decision, reason. record_grant: assetId, claimId, evidenceId, agreementId, agreementVersion. Decisions are verified, rejected, or ambiguous. Use the returned IDs and hashes; do not copy them across assets. Rights terms must include the exact platform, account_scope, territories, validity interval, duration limit, and permitted_actions analyze plus create_derivative only.
